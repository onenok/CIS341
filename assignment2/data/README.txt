Stanford Dogs, course copy for CIS341 Assignment 2

Images/            20,580 photos, one folder per breed (120 folders)
train_list.txt     12,000 photos: the dataset's official training list
test_list.txt      8,580 photos: the dataset's official test list

Paths in the lists are relative to Images/. Photos and split are unchanged from the original dataset; the lists were converted from its .mat files to plain text.

Credit: Aditya Khosla, Nityananda Jayadevaprakash, Bangpeng Yao and Li Fei-Fei, "Novel Dataset for Fine-Grained Image Categorization", First Workshop on Fine-Grained Visual Categorization, CVPR 2011. http://vision.stanford.edu/aditya86/ImageNetDogs/
The photos and annotations come from ImageNet. For course use only.
